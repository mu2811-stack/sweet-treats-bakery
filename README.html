ITE 270 · Module 5 · Motion & Animation

  1. Open index.html in Chrome. No install or build step needed.
  2. Press M to open the Motion X-ray. It lists every animation on your page with its timing, and can slow everything down.
  3. Follow Part 8 of the lesson, Build your own shop page. Every step has a matching STEP comment in styles.css or script.js.
  4. Write a motion sentence for every animation in MOTION-LOG.md.
File What it is
index.html Page content and SVG storefront. Rename the shop and replace the menu.
styles.css Layout is done (Part 1). Motion is yours (Part 2, STEP blocks).
script.js Scaffolds for Steps 5, 6, 7 and 9.
motion-xray.js Teaching helper. Press M. Remove the script tag before publishing if you like.
MOTION-LOG.md Your motion sentences and checks. Submit it with your site.
